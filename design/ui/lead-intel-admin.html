How it works

Every rule in the product, in plain words: what runs, what it costs, and what it cannot do.

1 — Getting businesses in

A Google Maps search for one of your ICPs (admin only, costs money, price shown first), a CSV import, or one typed by hand. Duplicates are matched on identity rather than name, so the same business never enters twice.

2 — Reading their website

Up to four pages with a plain crawler, free. Where a site refuses that, a real browser opens it instead and the model is asked what the measurements miss — about 0.05¢ a page.

3 — The 64 checks

Six areas. A check that could not run counts for neither side, so a site nobody could read never looks like a site with no problems. 17 of the 64 have no source today and say so.

4 — The scores

Problem is 0.6 × the worst area plus 0.4 × the weighted average. Signals counts evidence they already spend. Both are measured; neither is editable.

5 — Priority

Your rules, in your order, first match wins. An unknown fact never passes a condition, and a lead matching no rule says so rather than silently sinking.

6 — Buying more
