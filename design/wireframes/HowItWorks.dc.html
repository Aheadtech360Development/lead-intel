How it works
Every rule in the product, in plain words.
In one sentence: we source local businesses, filter them down to your ICPs, analyse their website against your checklists, and only then buy anything extra.
1
The four stages: sourcing, filtering, analysis, enrichment
Sourcing and enrichment can cost money. Filtering and analysis never do.
