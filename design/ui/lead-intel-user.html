How this works

Six things, in the order they happen to a lead. No jargon, and nothing you have to remember.

Businesses arrive

Three ways. You can type one in, or import a spreadsheet — both free. The third way is a Google Maps search, which costs money per search and is run by the administrator, who then hands out what it finds. Administrator only

Their website gets read

A crawler opens up to four pages — home, contact, products, about — and reads what is there. For sites that refuse a crawler, a real browser opens them instead, keeps a screenshot, and notes anything broken. Free. If a page cannot be read, that is recorded as a reason, not as a fault with the business.

64 checks run

Grouped into six things that matter: their website, being found on Google, their reviews, how easy they are to contact, their social media, and their advertising. Each check has one answer: a problem, no problem, or nobody could answer it. That third answer is never turned into one of the first two.

Two scores come out

Problem is how much is wrong — weighted so that one badly broken thing counts for more than several small ones. Signals is whether they already spend money, which is what makes a bad website worth a phone call rather than a shrug. Both are arithmetic. Nobody can type either one in.

Priority sorts your day

A lot wrong and already spending is a 1. Spending with little wrong is a 2. A lot wrong with no sign of money is a 3. Everything else is a 4. Work down from the top.

You can buy more, when it is worth it

Three things cost money: their Google listing (3.5¢), their Instagram profile (0.26¢) and their Meta ads (11.6¢). You are always shown the total and who is billing it before anything is bought, and a failed lookup is never charged.

The one habit worth having

When a score looks wrong, open Checks & scores and look at how many of the 64 were answered. A business with 19 answers is not a better business than one with 52 — it is a less-looked-at one. The product never pretends otherwise, and neither should a call.
