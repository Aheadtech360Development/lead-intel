How a business gets its two scores

Checks roll into six areas. The problem score weights the worst area at 60% and the average of all six at 40% — one catastrophic problem beats being mediocre everywhere, because it gives the call something to open with.

problem = 0.6 × worst + 0.4 × weighted average
signals = roll-up of the 10 intent checks

How priority is decided

1Problem ≥ 55 and signals ≥ 35 — call these first
2Spends money, little wrong — harder sell
3Lots wrong, no sign of spending yet
4Little wrong, no spending — lowest priority

Where AI is used — and where it is not

Three optional places, all powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash through Google AI Studio: suggesting the words for an ICP, critiquing a page a browser has rendered, and writing the plain-words briefing on a record.

No score, priority, ICP or pitch is ever decided by a model. The model is shown measurements the system already holds; it is never asked to find a fact.

The three answers

Every value can be yes, no, or nobody has looked. The third is never quietly turned into one of the first two.
